Privacy, in plain words.

This policy explains how Gito handles your information when you write, draw, add photos, or dictate a note.

Privacy Policy · Effective November 10, 2026

Your notes are stored on your device.Gito does not operate a server to collect your notes or require an account. Optional dictation uses Apple’s speech recognition services, which may process audio on Apple’s servers.

01 · About Gito

Gito is a personal note-taking app developed by Vivek Chahal. It lets you create and edit notes, attach photos and drawings, use speech-to-text dictation, and customize note colors and backgrounds. This policy covers the Gito app and its widget.

02 · Your notes and local storage

Note titles, text, selected images, drawings, and note preferences are stored locally using Apple’s SwiftData framework. The app and widget may access the same local app-group storage to display your notes.

Gito does not upload your note database to a developer-operated server or provide its own cloud-sync service. Device backups managed by Apple or your computer may include app data, depending on your settings.

Gito does not require registration and does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not sell your personal information or use your notes for advertising.

03 · Optional permissions

You can use writing and drawing features without granting camera or microphone access. Additional features use the following system capabilities:

  • Photos: the system photo picker provides the images you choose to attach. Gito does not scan your photo library.
  • Camera: camera permission lets you take a photo to add to a note.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: when you start dictation and grant permission, microphone audio is passed to Apple’s Speech framework to produce text for your note. Depending on Apple’s service, device, and language support, audio may be sent to Apple for processing. Dictation is not guaranteed to run entirely on your device.

Gito does not maintain a developer-operated archive of dictation recordings. Transcribed text becomes part of your note. Apple’s processing is governed by Apple’s privacy policies. You can stop dictation in the app and manage permissions in iOS Settings.

04 · Sharing and other services

When you choose to share or export content, iOS sends the selected content to the app or destination you choose. That recipient’s privacy practices apply to the shared copy.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever information you include. We use that information to respond and provide support. Please avoid sending sensitive note content unless it is necessary for your request.

Apple may provide developers with diagnostic or TestFlight feedback information under your Apple settings and Apple’s applicable terms. Gito does not include a separate third-party crash-reporting service.

05 · Retention and deletion

Notes remain in the app’s local storage until you delete them or remove the app’s data. You can delete notes within Gito. Deleting the app through iOS removes its app data, subject to any remaining shared-container data and Apple’s system behavior; offloading an app can retain its data.

Backups and copies you have exported or shared are separate. Manage those through the relevant backup settings or receiving service. Because we do not hold your local note database, we cannot remotely read, recover, or delete those notes for you.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to address your request and meet applicable obligations. You can contact us to request deletion of correspondence we control.

06 · Security

Gito relies on Apple’s app sandbox and device security protections for local data. Keep your device and operating system secure, use a device passcode, and review any widgets that may display note content on a visible screen. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

07 · Children’s privacy

Gito does not ask users for their age or create user profiles. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through support, contact us so we can address it.

08 · This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use analytics scripts, advertising, or forms. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure the website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

09 · Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when Gito’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.
